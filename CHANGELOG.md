# Changelog

All notable design decisions are recorded here (newest first). Format: date — change — reason — owner.

## v0.3-M3 — 2026-11-08 (design-freeze candidate)

- Repository structured for OSH publication (native CAD, exports, BOM, docs, tests, licences) — M3 requirement&nbsp;  
- Drive coupling detailed: printed ADAPTER (Ø2.0 mm shaft bore, Ø10 mm journal, 3 mm hex socket) \+ CLAMP/PUSH quick release, 61700 bearing — secure, tool-free attachment change  
- Costed BOM: €20.64 estimated prototype cost (BOM 08 Nov 2026).  
- Licence files added: CERN-OHL-S-2.0 (LICENSE), CC BY-SA 4.0 and MIT (LICENSES/).

## v0.2-M2 — 2026-10-25

- Scope narrowed from "mix food" to "whisk/mix light liquids \+ foam milk".  
- Injection-moulded PP replaced by FDM printing for the prototype (FabLab constraint).  
- Splash protection and food-contact boundary added as explicit requirements.  
- Unvalidated claims (teardown time, resin saving) removed.
