# &nbsp;Assembly instructions (v0.3)

Tools: screwdriver, soldering iron (motor leads only), heat-gun or lighter . Time around 30–45 min first build.&nbsp;

1. **Inserts** — press the M2/M3 heat-set inserts into INNER\_MOUNT and OUTER\_SHELL.  
2. **Motor** — Solder the red (+) and black (-) motor leads, add heat-shrink, and attach a JST-PH plug. Slide the motor into the inner mount (it locks into place without glue).&nbsp;  
3. **Bearing** —Press the 61700 bearing into the inner mount and secure it with the retainer and seal.&nbsp;  
4. **Adapter** — Push the adapter onto the motor shaft through the bearing, then attach the adapter cap.&nbsp;  
5. **Quick-release** —Insert the spring and push button, then snap the clamp into place. Make sure the button springs back on its own.&nbsp;  
6. **Electronics** — Mount the charger, PWM module, and button onto the board using the JST plugs and wiring guide&nbsp;  
7. **Inner assembly** — Slide the inner assembly into the outer shell, align the USB-C port with the opening, and secure with screws.&nbsp;  
8. **Battery** —Insert the 18650 cell (positive side matching the mark) and close the outer cap with its seal.&nbsp;  
9. **Function check** —Charge via USB-C (LED changes from red to green), press the button to run for 10 seconds, and test the attachment mechanism by pushing a hex shank and pulling to verify its locks.&nbsp;
