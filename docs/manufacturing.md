# Manufacturing (FabLab)

| Part | Material | Layer | Walls | Infill | Supports | Orientation / note |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| OUTER\_SHELL | PETG | 0.2 mm | 4 (≥1.6 mm) | 20 % gyroid | as needed | upright; seam on back side |

| CLAMP, PUSH | PETG | 0.12 mm | 3 | 100 % | no | print 2 spares (wear parts) |

| BOARD | PETG | 0.2 mm | 3 | 30 % | no | flat |

Printer: any FDM printer with 0.4 mm nozzle, PETG 230–245 °C , bed 75–85 °C. Heat-set inserts: soldering iron at \~220 °C. Tools: PH1 screwdriver, flush cutter, soldering iron, multimeter.&nbsp;

Why PETG: printable in the FabLab, tougher and more heat-tolerant than PLA, mono-material (recyclable stream). Printed parts are kept **outside the food-contact zone**.
