# User guide
- **Charge** with any USB-C 5 V charger; LED red = charging, green = full.
- **Attach** a head: push the hex shank in until it clicks. **Release**: press the button on the coupling and pull.
- **Use**: immerse the head before switching on; press the button to start/change speed; switch off before lifting out (prevents splashing).
- **Frother**: 100–200 ml milk, 20–40 s. **Whisk**: eggs, cream, light dressings — not dough or thick batter.
- **Clean**: remove the head and rinse/wash it (dishwasher OK for stainless heads). Wipe the body with a damp cloth — **do not immerse the body**.
- **Safety**: keep fingers and hair away from rotating heads; do not use a swollen or damaged cell; children only under supervision.
